01

Air Pollution: Types, Sources & Classification

Primary vs secondary pollutants, criteria pollutants, smog, acid rain

Air pollution: Presence of chemicals in atmosphere in sufficient quantities/duration to (1) cause harm, (2) alter climate.
Primary pollutants: Directly emitted (CO, SO₂, NOₓ, SPM, VOCs).
Secondary pollutants: Formed by chemical reactions (ozone, PAN, H₂O₂, sulfuric/nitric acid).
Analogy: Primary = raw ingredients (flour, eggs). Secondary = baked cake (chemical reaction in oven = atmosphere). You regulate ingredients to control the cake.

Figure 1: Air pollution — primary/secondary pollutants, criteria pollutants, smog, acid deposition

Worked Example: Photochemical Smog in Delhi

  1. Primary: NOₓ, VOCs from vehicles, industry (morning rush)
  2. Reaction: Sunlight + NOₓ + VOCs → O₃, PAN, aldehydes
  3. Secondary: O₃ peaks afternoon; PAN causes eye irritation
  4. Control: Reduce NOₓ/VOCs (BS-VI norms, EV transition, industrial controls)
Exam sentence: "Primary pollutants are emitted; secondary pollutants are manufactured in the atmospheric reactor. Control the precursors, not just the products."
Trap: "Ozone is always bad." Stratospheric ozone = good (UV shield). Tropospheric ozone = bad (smog, health damage). Same molecule, different altitude.
60-second teach-back: Primary vs secondary with examples

Primary (direct): CO, SO₂, NOₓ, SPM (dust, asbestos), VOCs (methane, CFCs)
Secondary (formed): O₃, PAN, H₂O₂, H₂SO₄, HNO₃
Criteria (NAAQS): CO, NO₂, SO₂, SPM, O₃, Pb, Hydrocarbons
Hazardous (TRIC): Toxic, Reactive, Ignitable, Corrosive

02

Smog, Acid Deposition & Ozone Depletion

Industrial smog (gray): SO₂ + particulates + humidity → sulfuric acid aerosols. Coal burning.
Photochemical smog: NOₓ + VOCs + sunlight → ozone, PAN, aldehydes. Vehicle emissions.
Acid deposition: SO₂ + NOₓ → H₂SO₄ + HNO₃ → wet (rain/snow) + dry (particles).
Ozone depletion: CFCs → stratosphere → Cl/Br catalytic destruction of O₃. Antarctic hole (1985).
Analogy: CFCs = time bombs. Stable in lower atmosphere, but UV in stratosphere breaks them → Cl atoms → each Cl destroys 100,000+ O₃ molecules before being deactivated. Montreal Protocol = disarming the bombs.

Figure 2: Smog types, acid deposition pathways, ozone depletion mechanism

Exam sentence: "Smog is not one thing: gray = coal/industry (SO₂), brown = cars/sunlight (NOₓ+VOCs). Acid rain travels hundreds of km — transboundary by nature."
Trap: "Acid rain" ≠ only rain. Dry deposition (acidic particles/gases) = 30-50% of total acid input. Also, ozone depletion ≠ greenhouse effect — CFCs cause BOTH but mechanisms differ.
60-second teach-back: Smog types + acid deposition + ozone

Industrial smog: Gray, SO₂ + particulates, coal, humidity
Photochemical smog: Brown, NOₓ + VOCs + sun, ozone, PAN
Acid deposition: SO₂+NOₓ → H₂SO₄/HNO₃, wet + dry
Ozone depletion: CFCs → stratosphere → Cl catalytic O₃ destruction, Antarctic hole

03

Water Pollution, Groundwater & Hazardous Waste

Point sources: Specific locations (pipes, ditches, sewers) → easier to control.
Non-point sources: Diffuse, untraceable (agricultural runoff, urban stormwater) → difficult to control.
Groundwater pollution: Out-of-sight, low ecological risk but high health risk, slow degradation (low flow, few bacteria, cold).
Hazardous waste (TRIC): Toxic, Reactive, Ignitable, Corrosive.
Ocean pollution: Harmful Algal Blooms (HAB) from sewage + agricultural runoff.
Analogy: Point source = leaking pipe (find it, fix it). Non-point = thousand tiny leaks across a watershed (can't plug them all). Groundwater = slow-motion disaster — contamination today shows up in wells 20 years later.

Figure 3: Water pollution types, groundwater characteristics, hazardous waste TRIC

Worked Example: Bellandur Lake, Bangalore

  1. Problem: Methane build-up on surface → caught fire (2015, 2017, 2018)
  2. Cause: Untreated sewage (500+ MLD) + industrial effluents → anaerobic decomposition → methane
  3. Pollutants: High BOD, COD, ammonia, heavy metals, phosphates
  4. Response: STP upgrades, wetland restoration, foam control, but recurrence persists
Exam sentence: "Groundwater pollution is the ultimate intergenerational externality: invisible, persistent, and the bill comes due decades later when the aquifer is the only water left."
Trap: "Non-point source" ≠ "uncontrollable." Best management practices (BMPs): buffer strips, contour farming, constructed wetlands, urban green infrastructure. Difficult ≠ impossible.
60-second teach-back: Water pollution classification + groundwater

Point: Pipes, ditches, sewers → traceable → controllable
Non-point: Runoff, atmospheric deposition → diffuse → hard to control
Groundwater: Out-of-sight, slow flow, few bacteria, cold = persistent
TRIC waste: Toxic, Reactive, Ignitable, Corrosive
HAB: Algal blooms from sewage + ag runoff

Module 7 Practice MCQs

M7-1. Which pollutant is a SECONDARY air pollutant?

Ozone (O₃) is formed by photochemical reactions of NOₓ and VOCs in sunlight — a classic secondary pollutant. CO, SO₂, SPM are emitted directly (primary).">
✓ Correct Answer: Ozone (O₃)

M7-2. Photochemical smog is characterized by:

Photochemical smog = NOₓ + VOCs + sunlight → ozone, PAN, aldehydes (brown haze). Industrial smog (gray) = SO₂ + particulates + humidity. The two are distinct in chemistry and sources.">
✓ Correct Answer: NOₓ, VOCs, sunlight → ozone, PAN, aldehydes

M7-3. The TRIC classification for hazardous waste stands for:

TRIC = Toxic, Reactive, Ignitable, Corrosive. These are the four characteristics defining hazardous waste under RCRA and similar frameworks. Radioactive waste is regulated separately.">
✓ Correct Answer: Toxic, Reactive, Ignitable, Corrosive

M7-4. Groundwater pollution is characterized by:

Groundwater = out-of-sight, low flow rates, few bacteria, cold temperatures → very slow degradation. Low ecological risk (not visible), but HIGH health risk (drinking water). Degradation takes decades. 'Out-of-sight, out-of-mind' until wells are contaminated.">
✓ Correct Answer: Low ecological risk, high health risk, slow degradation

M7-5. Harmful Algal Blooms (HABs) are caused by:

HABs = explosive growth of harmful algae fueled by nutrient pollution (nitrogen, phosphorus) from sewage and agricultural runoff. Eutrophication → algal blooms → toxins → fish kills, shellfish poisoning, dead zones. Not caused by thermal discharge or oil directly.">
✓ Correct Answer: Explosive growth of harmful algae from sewage and agricultural runoff
